## Pricing and purchase preview

The public price cards and dependency-inclusive summaries read the existing `system-services.ts` and centralized `service-pricing.ts` values in MYR integer sen. No separate payable product catalog was invented.

- Free Core: RM0, including one website property with multiple pages, basic catalog/company tools and 20 MiB free storage. Original trial storage is 50 MiB. Premium design and higher Storage entitlements remain separate.
- Product POS: proposed RM29/month/company.
- Orders & Kitchen Queue: proposed RM29/month/company, requiring Product POS. Both order tracking and kitchen queue belong to this one Orders service. **POS + Orders = RM58/month**, not RM87; examples derive from the same pricing definitions.
- Cake Pre-orders: proposed RM29; Appointment Booking: proposed RM39; Staff Scheduling: proposed RM19/month/company. All three are Coming Soon, cannot be selected for purchase, and have disabled actions.

The summary covers actual scope, company licensing/roles, proposed billing period, activation/expiry, maintenance/security, general support, exclusions, manual renewal, cancellation/refund policy, data retention/export and linked draft terms/privacy. A one-month preview is explicitly defined as one **calendar month** from verified activation to the corresponding date/time next month in UTC, clamped to the final day when needed; it is not an undefined month or a 30-day promise. No dates or entitlement are issued now. A future issuer must display exact timestamps before accepting money and implement/test that contract, renewals and maintenance scope rather than silently choosing a different period.

The active-period maintenance scope is proposed hosted-service access and generally released maintenance/security updates, pending final review. General product support is through configured channels only. No 24/7 coverage, response-time/SLA guarantee, custom feature development or refund/hardware implementation is promised. Refund eligibility/windows, cancellation rights and all final commercial terms remain visibly unresolved until human/legal review.

Purchase state is local React state only. There is no purchase form submission, server purchase action, checkout session, payment SDK, webhook, paid entitlement creation, verified billing record or automatic renewal. Terms acknowledgement is not persisted as contractual acceptance and never enables checkout. The existing authenticated demo **design** unlock behavior was not changed by this public service preview. The anonymous estimate does not inspect a company's existing paid services or calculate proration; a future trusted checkout must reconcile existing licenses and their periods before collecting money.

## Contact, legal and trust content

`SERVQOO_CONTACT_EMAIL` and `SERVQOO_WHATSAPP_NUMBER` are optional business-supplied public configuration. Invalid/missing email or non-international-digits phone values are hidden. Email opens the visitor's email client; WhatsApp opens the configured channel with no message sent by the application. No credentials or user submissions are logged.

No contact form was added, so no misleading submit/success/delivery UI exists. A future form needs server validation, durable throttling, bot protection, privacy/retention information and bounded verified delivery with safe errors before it can be enabled. Resend/SMTP were not configured. Existing Supabase verification/recovery email behavior is preserved.

Legal content lives centrally in `src/lib/legal-content.ts`. Required business input includes operator/entity name, address, applicable registration details, jurisdiction, effective date and legal/privacy/support contacts. Final terms need reviewed acceptable-use/IP and liability/dispute provisions; final price/tax/invoice and service-period handling; cancellation/refund eligibility/procedure; retention, account deletion and backup deletion; discontinuation notice/export windows and remaining paid-period treatment. No legal numbers or business contacts were invented. Documents visibly remain drafts and noindex until reviewed; publication here is not legal approval.

## SEO, intended domain and redirects

`servqoo.com` is an intended domain only; ownership, DNS and HTTPS are unverified and unchanged. Optional `SERVQOO_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` controls public canonical/Open Graph URLs. Blank falls back only to an existing valid HTTPS `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` for metadata; it does not write Auth settings. No valid origin means no canonical or sitemap entries and disabled indexing. Vercel preview environments also remain non-indexed.

`SERVQOO_REDIRECT_FROM_HOSTS` is blank/off by default. If explicitly configured later with verified old hosts and a valid target, redirects cover public marketing paths only, preserve query strings and exclude the target host to avoid loops. Auth/login/signup, Dashboard, API and tenant site URLs are not redirected by this helper. No production redirect or environment value was set.

[Public go-live instructions](docs/PUBLIC-GO-LIVE.md) document future ownership verification, exact Vercel-provided DNS records/HTTPS, apex/www selection, canonical environment setup, separate coordinated Supabase Site URL/callback/recovery changes and real email/session tests. They were checked against [Vercel domain setup](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/working-with-domains/add-a-domain), [Vercel domain redirects](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/working-with-domains/deploying-and-redirecting) and [Supabase redirect URL configuration](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/redirect-urls). No DNS records were guessed or configured. No email sender, SMTP, Resend, analytics pixel or social account integration was added.
